# UKMT-SENIOR-MATHEMATICAL-CHALLENGE

## My First UKMT Senior Mathematical Challenge

- **The competition was on:** 07/10/2026
- **How did I participate:** Through King's College (Alicante), which hosted the competition
- **Result:** Waiting for my score (the score will be updated when it is given)

## Preparation

To prepare for this competition, after hours of research, practice, and asking questions, I worked through a wide range of challenging mathematical topics and resources.

From **Brilliant**, I completed courses such as *Logic, Truth & Lies, Reasoning with Logic, Number Theory,* and *Bayesian Probability*. From **Mathsaurus**, I studied *Prime Factorization, Trailing Zeroes,* and *Permutations*. I also watched videos focused on **UKMT Maths Challenges** to become more familiar with the style and difficulty of the problems.

Through **Art of Problem Solving (AoPS) Alcumus**, I worked on *Prealgebra + Introduction to Algebra, Number Theory, Counting & Probability,* and *Geometry*, specifically focusing on the subtopics of *Triangles, Polygons,* and *Circles*.

I also used **Dr. Frost** to practise *Number Theory, Combinatorics,* and *Geometry/Shape*, as well as the **UKMT Enrichment Database – Senior**.

Finally, I completed **Diagnostic Questions UKMT**, including **Past Papers&#x20;**&#x73;ince 2020 - 2025, with all of these resources coming from or being connected to the official UKMT materials.

## How Did It Go?

When I first entered the competition, I felt a mixture of **nervousness, confidence, and determination**. I felt prepared to confront the paper, although once it was handed to me, the nerves started to kick in. Fortunately, they did not affect my concentration.

During the exam, I experienced a mixture of emotions: I was sometimes **confused**, sometimes **relieved**, and sometimes **confident**. The last three questions were particularly challenging in this year's paper. I was looking forward to completing them, but unfortunately, I wasn't able to.

I think I performed pretty well on the other questions, and by the end of the competition, I felt as if **a weight had been lifted off my shoulders**.

## What to Improve

For the next competition, I would need to improve my **Geometry** and my ability to tackle the **last three questions**, as I believe these are currently my weakest areas, even though I am working to improve them every day.

I want to become more confident when facing unfamiliar and challenging problems, especially when they require several different ideas or techniques to reach a solution.
